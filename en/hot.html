How is heat calculated?

Each item is first scored by rules: base 50 points; freshness (+25 within 6 hours, +20 within 24 hours, +10 within 72 hours); Official sources +10; T3 sources -15; each topic keyword hit (pregao, credenciamento, procurement, tender, bidding) +5, capped at +25; each noise keyword 0, floor 0; total capped at 100. Heat = score × 0.8^days (decaying daily), looking back 30 days only. Trends compare with the previous list: up 2 or more places counts as rising, down 2 or more as cooling, a 1-place move as steady; first-time entries are marked “New”.

Brazil Procurement HOT is an aggregator and reading index. Titles and summaries belong to their original sources. Content is for reference only; full text is never republished.

Data comes from PNCP, Brazil's national public procurement portal (pncp.gov.br). Titles and summaries here are rewritten in our own words; no official notice text is republished.
